passengershipdesign.net

In times of change a renaissance of passenger shipping seems within reach:

Passengershipdesign.net is an initiative to inspire new design solutions exploiting the dormant potential of passenger ships as a sustainable means for transportation or tourism. Emerging technologies allow for a dramatic reduction or even elimination of harmful emissions. Responsible itineraries and appropriately sized ships can enable sustainable traveling with minimal negative impact.
